# PER-RT-0209 — Tenant / Renter (Flexible Terms)

**Role:** Business renting storage space.
**Lens:** Fairness, predictability, no surprise charges.

## Key concerns
- Wants to start paying only when actually using the space (possession), not when signing.
- May want indefinite/month-to-month terms without a forced end date.
- Partial-month charges must be transparent (per-day or per-month proration stated).
- Deposit should be refundable and tracked separately from rent.

## Acceptance criteria
- Billing start date can be later than agreement date.
- Indefinite leases generate monthly ledgers until terminated.
- Proration method and amount visible on the ledger entry.
- Deposit shown as a distinct receivable, not mixed into rent.
