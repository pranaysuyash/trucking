## Explicit unresolved inputs and design limits

- PR-12 cannot yet answer whether the additional $170 saves or costs four hours. The input fixture supplies a magnitude but no direction or absolute durations. A product scenario must supply those fields before a complete service tradeoff is claimed.
- TR-06 explains the arithmetic total but cannot yet attribute the 18 points to individual policy/review factors. That allocation and its sources must be supplied before factor attribution is claimed.
- These technical studies are first review candidates. The six generated concepts explore a higher finish and are not implemented screens. Neither the earlier local ten-minute study nor Sael is treated as an approved quality ceiling.
- Exact numeric fixture editing and actual pointer/keyboard dimension handles work in CF-02 and AS-05, with undo/redo, Escape cancellation and reset. Generalized controls do not perform case-specific operational actions.
- Browser emulation supports the responsive comparison. Physical-device performance, keyboard equivalence, complete accessibility and production renderer budgets remain unverified. Some dense scenes use thousands of draw calls; this isolated package does not establish a shipping performance budget.
- The approval interface binds decisions to source, responsive-image bytes and the submitted fixture/layer context, and guards response ownership. The agent has not created a successful human decision as a test. Pranay's explicit review remains the next creative decision.
