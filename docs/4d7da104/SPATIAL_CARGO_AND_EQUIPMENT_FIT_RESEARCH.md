# CargoFit Spatial Inspection & Load Planning Architecture Research

## 1. Executive Summary & Vision
This document outlines the architectural research, mathematical heuristics, and interactive UX paradigms for:
1. Interactive 3D/Isometric cargo inspection with spatial rotation and allowed-orientation previews.
2. 3D volumetric vehicle and equipment wireframes with door bottleneck and axle reference overlays.
3. Multi-strategy heuristic comparison (Heavy-First vs FIFO Stop-Order vs Tight-Wall Packing).
4. State-of-the-art bin packing heuristics and physical safety bounds.
